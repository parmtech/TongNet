# TongNet — Prior Art & Novelty Research Ledger

**Status:** NOT a completed worldwide novelty search. Do not claim that the complete TongNet idea was never previously published.

| Relevant existing concept | Overlap with TongNet | Research still needed |
|---|---|---|
| Hash/time-lock contracts (HTLCs) | Conditional payment claim / timed refunds | Compare exact two-phase trigger, windows, inbox distribution and reserve behaviour |
| Atomic swaps/payment channels | Payment conditionality and settlement | Look for receiver secret, sender cancel constraints, fraud-proof finality |
| Multisig and account abstraction | Multiple authorisation keys | Compare independent inbox second factor and stolen wallet scenarios |
| TOTP (RFC 6238) | 60-second rotating login codes | Study phishing resistance, replay, device compromise and time sync |
| E2E-encrypted distributed messaging | Private sender/receiver inbox messages | Availability, addressing, metadata, deletion and key exchange |
| Threshold signatures/quorums | Witness-based transaction finality | Analyze trusted membership/Sybil model and Byzantine fault assumptions |
| Federated ledgers and DAG designs | Alternative ledger topology | Examine validator governance and payment finality without mining/PoS |
| Lost/unclaimed property laws | Proposed 96-hour expiry and reserve forfeiture | Legal rights, consent, escrow or trust obligations, jurisdiction |

## Method

- Search scholarly publications, code implementations, RFCs, open patents and relevant standards.
- Record exact URLs, publication dates, claim-by-claim comparisons and methodology.
- Identify *precise* technical contributions, not just combinations of familiar nouns.
- Seek intellectual-property and regulatory advice where relevant.

**Nothing here represents a patentability opinion.**
