# TongNet — Reciprocal Transaction Validation (PoRV): zero user fee and transaction-powered consensus feasibility

**Date:** 2026-10-25, Australia/Sydney. **Status:** Technical feasibility/risk research **ONLY**, not an owner-approved consensus rule, deployment, code, benchmark, or a proven attack-proof invention. The owner asks for an exclusively transaction-participant-operated network, no dedicated miners/validators, with users validating other users' INITIATE, CLAIM_REQUEST and Sender BlackBox FINAL_ACCEPT, in reciprocation for their own near-zero-fee transactions. Example: ~10 peers each contribute about 1/10th of a transaction's service cost; no cash fees. Must prevent fake wallets/self-deals/collusion. All earlier requirements for single native TONG, no public dApp platform, manual BlackBox sender final ACCEPT, first receiver 24h then sender 60 full days, and ACTIVE Recovery Mirror retained.

## Executive finding — important impossibility/tradeoff boundary

**These four requirements CANNOT simultaneously be guaranteed for a fully permissionless network in all circumstances:**
1. Any unlimited free newly created wallet receives equal consensus power with no externally scarce resource or trustworthy uniqueness registration;
2. all operations require zero coin fee, no minimum work/stake, and admit unlimited malicious submissions;
3. NO continuously available nodes/service providers are needed and validation runs **only** while each party is already doing its own transaction;
4. arbitrary fake-identity resistance, safe single ledger history, continuous 24/7 liveness and instant finality, no matter how many users are offline or malicious.

In a network with arbitrary free identities the attacker can create vastly more committee identities and capture random 10-of-10 approvals; including an INITIATE, CLAIM_REQUEST and FINAL_ACCEPT which are all signed by attacker-held wallets. **Three signatures do not equal independent network witnesses.** Where no honest third-party peer is online, a sender and receiver alone cannot reach decentralized agreement on conflicting history with a meaningful BFT fault tolerance. If no users are online, no new approval can be network-finalised, though the chain's prior blocks remain as stored data.

No cryptographic system honestly guarantees 'cannot be misused in any way'; specify bounded threat assumptions. A scarce Sybil-resistance cost (work/stake/human uniqueness with issuer-trust conditions) and *some* ongoing independent availability are unavoidable under these goals. User-facing zero TONG transaction fee does not make energy/storage/bandwidth costs disappear; the cost is borne in service credits, volunteer infrastructure, subsidy or issuance, and can be unbalanced across participants.

## Existing technically grounded patterns (not to claim invention)
- **Nano:** zero user monetary fee, small transaction PoW, balance bucket/least-recently-used queuing, independent Open Representative Voting. https://docs.nano.org/protocol-design/spam-work-and-prioritization/ and https://docs.nano.org/protocol-design/orv-consensus/ . Nano says nodes have no direct fee reward and are run for the usefulness of the network: https://nano.org/
- **Algorand:** VRF sortition samples online voters from registered economic stake/participation keys; vote/committee formation distinct from routine wallet transactions. Splitting stake into identities does not multiply stake-weighted chance; it still entails stake concentration, quorum and online assumptions. https://dev.algorand.co/concepts/protocol/overview/ ; https://github.com/algorand/go-algorand/blob/master/docs/agreement_service.md
- **Avalanche:** randomized repeated subsample voting across validator nodes, but its public chains enforce PoS Sybil protection, not "one free wallet = one vote." https://docs.avax.network/docs/nodes/architecture/consensus and https://docs.avax.network/docs/nodes/configure/configs-flags
- **BFT (>2/3 quorum):** 10 signer slots and a 7-of-10 commit **under a specifically analyzed BFT protocol** can at best tolerate 3 Byzantine participants if they represent actual independent bounded voting power (n>=3f+1). Ten attacker-controlled keypairs are not ten honest independent persons; a simple one-round multi-signature with 7-of-10 is not automatically a safe BFT implementation. See https://github.com/cometbft/cometbft/blob/main/spec/consensus/consensus.md .
- **Proof of useful service:** Filecoin data-possession/proof-of-spacetime protocols can attest storage without a trusted statement that someone worked; validation vote receipts can attest a signature but not the honest expense of a mobile participant. https://docs.filecoin.cloud/core-concepts/pdp-overview ; https://spec.filecoin.io/algorithms/pos/

## Strongest feasible *near* interpretation: Reciprocal Validation Pool (research prototype, not confirmed)

### A. Network operation and participation
- EVERY willing ordinary TONG user may **opt into a small independent participation node** on mobile/desktop/server, registering a separate short-lived service key; spending key and BlackBox secrets never leave wallet/device. In-app FULL validation requires ledger verification and accessible state; weak devices may use a verified light client only and cannot dishonestly claim full-node consensus work.
- Transaction actions create **service obligations / work opportunities**: participating clients verify previously selected unrelated escrow blocks, signatures and chain-state commitments **for other people**, earning *nontransferable service credits* if independently assigned and accepted. Avoid extra "coin/token" issuance: accounting credits only track valid service or access priority, NEVER consensus voting power by itself, NEVER a spendable second currency.
- An independently selected rotating quorum must be **online when processing each block**, including periods with few payments. Some users must choose to stay online outside their own payment; intermittent nodes can earn credits for shifts, but if the pool is unavailable, **chain must wait rather than pay on a weak or fabricated quorum**. No hidden trusted fallback admin or single company validator. This relaxes the owner's strictly transactions-only scheduling ambition, but is necessary for liveness and meaningful adversarial finality.

### B. Economic scarcity and fake-account resistance
- A cheap new wallet must NOT automatically get consensus voting weight. Economic collateral / refundable duty bond for OPT-IN validator participants is one defensible Sybil cost to evaluate; it is not a transaction fee charged to all users. Dynamic capped committee weights and independent-proof personhood are possible further experiments but one human per wallet is not provable from fingerprint/mobile alone. Bound weight by an economically scarce stake/collateral set using a prior committed snapshot. Stake concentration is a separate, unsolved plutocracy risk; cross-cloud node diversity cannot be guaranteed without privacy tradeoffs.
- Commitment/register validator keys before the randomness for future committee selection is knowable; choose by VRF/random beacon built from prior finalized epoch and previously bonded participants, **NOT controllable by sender nonce, receiver code or user's transaction hash**. The sender/receiver and traceably related participant identities should not decide their own transfer, but complete real-world independence cannot be proven with pseudonyms.
- A committee of "10 random wallets" is a **visual example only**, not a finalized safe parameter. Consider 3f+1 BFT threshold and committee capture probabilities across epoch weights, packet loss and refusal to participate; mandatory peer replacement/fail-closed behaviors. Operators may use multiple wallet identities but bonded vote weight is constrained by scarce capital, not counting aliases.
- Slashing only for cryptographically provable misbehavior such as double-signing conflicting BFT votes or signing deterministically invalid state (with evidence). Not being online or temporarily ill is not inherently fraud; rules need separate availability policy.

### C. Transaction pipeline, TongNet BlackBox integration
1. S `INITIATE` signed by S Wallet spending key; dedicated BlackBox creates high-entropy K_R privately. A small device-computable bounded work challenge may rate-limit abusive INITIATE. Validators independently check exact recipient binding, account spendability, validity and singleness, escrow LOCK, and network-finalise state. NO validator sees plaintext K_R; the encrypted S BlackBox mirror goes to its currently active recovery target under the latest Active Mirror rule.
2. R `CLAIM_REQUEST` is signed by the designated receiver wallet and contains privacy-preserving proof/secure verification of knowledge of K_R (no raw on-chain code). R's opted-in device might validate an unrelated old finalized-state checkpoint and return signed service receipt; receipt does not count toward own claim consensus. Validator committee checks eligibility but does NOT pay R yet.
3. S BlackBox `FINAL_ACCEPT` signs immutable TX, recipient, amount, request ID, expiry and nonce. This proof goes to independently selected eligible witnesses; their distinct domain-separated consensus keys form a valid multi-round finality certificate BEFORE adjusted receiver deadline. No sender acceptance => no receiver settlement. Minimize overhead in this time-critical step. Sender K_S after receiver priority and following 60-day reclaim constraints unchanged and still require a lost-BlackBox solution.
4. Final settlement receipt (verifiable BFT certificate, state root and sequence) is returned to both clients and archived **inside S BlackBox**, then encrypted-parent mirrored according to registered recovery pairing. Do not mirror on-chain historical transactions as inherited ledger entries. Witnesses get only required public proofs, not parent-archive private contents.

### D. Reciprocity / 'ten persons ten cents of service'
- Example accounting: estimated transaction verification is 10 abstract SERVICE UNITS; 10 qualifying witnesses contribute one service unit each. Units **DO NOT equal 10 cents** of real cost: CPU, networking, full-ledger storage, backup replication and availability differ by participant and time. Estimates must come from benchmark measurements not equal-split assertions.
- An action can earn **service credits only for verifiable work on independently assigned OTHER people's jobs** (signed BFT votes, verified storage challenge and auditable networking commitments). No automatic rewards for creating own INITIATE, self-transfer, creating wallet, clicking Accept, or merely claiming to validate. Generic validation signature alone cannot prove costly honest work or retrievability.
- Credits can influence fair-use priority/quota, but should not grant more consensus voting power or mint TONG automatically. Outstanding work obligation should be asynchronous/cleared over periods, **not** a recursive "validate another tx before my tx can complete, which requires someone else first" deadlock. There must be a bootstrap/newcomer path with guarded limited allowance, but unlimited free newcomer allowances are Sybil bait. Usage quotas, lightweight bound client puzzle and congestion priorities help; none prove human uniqueness.
- When validation demand does NOT match traffic (nighttime / asymmetric heavy senders vs online service workers) costs cannot vanish by a mutual-credit ledger. Options: volunteer operators (Nano-inspired), transparent capped user-approved coin issuance/subsidy from legitimately financed reserve, optional validator-only collateral rewards, or reduced QoS. Avoid funding network from owner-proposed seizure of unclaimed user escrows until jurisdiction-specific legal/safety review.

### E. Abuse and fail-closed test suite
1. Attacker spins up 100,000 wallets and self-deals: no election power from identity count; no service credit from self-attesting tx; bounded spam channels; inspect stake/bond attack economics.
2. Attacker buys/rents/colludes with enough actual bonded voting weight: model 1/3 BFT threshold; acknowledge capture possibility and slash limitations.
3. Selected witness group is offline / split during phone lock/sleep or outage: rotate/timeout to already registered active pool without exceeding fault bounds, or fail closed. Never auto-pay from insufficient quorum.
4. 0 transaction arrival for many hours and a lone sender later starts new transaction: validator pool/checkpoint/peer data must remain online for low-traffic finality, or latency cannot be guaranteed. Empty heartbeat/checkpoint messages are network work beyond transactions.
5. Two conflicting S spends from partitioned network: state-root ordering, lock proofs, replay protection and multi-round BFT finality; a sender and receiver signing both cannot choose a canonical history.
6. Adaptive PoW and fair queue while under 1m spam requests/min; protect weaker phones and genuine microtransactions; defense should not accidentally gate disabled users or those with no funds.
7. Abuse of BlackBox secret storage, K_R offline guessing oracle, forged sender FINAL_ACCEPT, early decrypt of recovery mirror, chain status DEAD / auto-unbind behavior.
8. Sybil capture of randomness (validator registration after seed, sender hash grind), equivocation/double sign evidence, cloud-provider geographic concentration, low initial stake participation, expensive fee-free node storage.

## Decision / falsifiable recommendation
**No design currently supports the owner's combination of "literally only transaction events ever produce all validation", "no ongoing online participants", "free unlimited identities with equal votes", "zero user costs" and "guaranteed impossible to misuse."** Don't label that as proven or implement false safety.

Prototype a **single-coin TONG permissionless reciprocal service layer** atop a **Sybil-resistant rotating user-operated committee consensus pool**, no mandatory professional mining farm, no fee displayed to ordinary users under bounded-use policies, nontransferable verified-service ledger but NOT extra cryptocurrency, and intact manual three-step BlackBox authorization. Everyone may contribute real node work voluntarily; participating witness nodes still *are validators* in the technical sense. A limited independent active set and economic/identity scarcity must exist, and user fairness requires benchmarking, long-run costs and committee security proof.

**Owner decisions remain OPEN:** (1) willingness to require opt-in user nodes to be online between transfers; (2) whether validators may lock a refundable bond or whether the owner forbids **all** scarce resource requirements; (3) whether free monetary fees but bounded fair-use work/priority is acceptable; (4) governance on emission/donations/subsidies for 24/7 availability. Do not silently replace original hybrid BFT with this proposal without approval.
